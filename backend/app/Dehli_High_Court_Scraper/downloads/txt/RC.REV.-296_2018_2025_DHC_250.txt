Learned Counsel for the Petitioner/tenant submits that this offer is acceptable to the Petitioner/tenant.
3.	The Petitioner/tenant agrees to pay lump-sum user and occupation charges in the sum of Rs.40,000/- on or before 31.03.2025.

(i)	The Petitioners/tenant shall hand over the vacant physical possession of the premises i.e., shop bearing No.C/67, Shivaji Park, New Delhi as shown in red color in the site plan annexed with the Eviction Petition [hereinafter referred to as �subject premises�].

(iii)	The lump-sum use and occupation charges shall be paid by the Petitioner/tenant in the sum of Rs.40,000/- shall be paid on or before 31.03.2025;
(iv)	The Petitioner/tenant undertakes and confirms that the entire subject premises is under his occupation and control; 
(v)	The Petitioner/tenant will pay all the utility bills such as electricity and water and any other dues, for the subject premises till the date of handing over of the vacant, physical and peaceful possession thereof;
